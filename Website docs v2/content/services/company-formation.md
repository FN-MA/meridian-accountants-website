**Setting up a limited company is quick, but getting the details right at the start saves problems later.** Meridian Accountants can advise whether a company is right for you, form the company, verify your identity for Companies House and register it with HMRC.

Meridian Accountants Limited is an **Authorised Corporate Service Provider (ACSP)**, so we can complete Companies House identity verification for directors and people with significant control.

## What's included

- **Advice on structure**: whether a limited company suits you better than staying a sole trader, and how to split shares
- **Company formation** at Companies House, including your articles of association and share structure
- **Identity verification** for directors and people with significant control, as an ACSP
- **Registrations with HMRC**: corporation tax, and PAYE and VAT if you need them

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to set up a limited company?

Once identity verification is done and the details are agreed, Companies House usually registers a company online within a day or two.

### Do I need to verify my identity with Companies House?

Yes. Directors and people with significant control must now verify their identity with Companies House. As an Authorised Corporate Service Provider, we can do this for you as part of setting up the company.

### How much share capital should I put in?

Often only a small amount, with any further money you put in recorded as a loan to the company. That loan can usually be repaid to you tax-free later. Our [director's loan account guide](https://meridian-accountants.com/blog/directors-loan-account-explained/) explains how this works.

### Can I use your address as my registered office?

Get in touch to discuss what you need. Many directors don't want their home address on the public register, and we can talk through the options.
