About the Firm

Built on Expertise,
Guided by Integrity

Meridian Accountants was founded by Farid Nasri on a simple, personal conviction: providing genuine financial expertise to businesses without the price tag that comes with it. We are an ambitious firm, and we want to work with ambitious, growing clients — not just at month-end or year-end, but to be involved in your business day to day, understanding what's happening as it happens to be able to advise you in real time.

Farid is an ACA qualified Chartered Accountant, holding the prestigious designation awarded by the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW). This rigorous qualification — and our ongoing regulation by the ICAEW — means you can engage our services with complete confidence in our professional standards and ethical commitments.

Whether you are a sole trader just starting out, a growing SME, or an established company seeking a more personal service, we bring the same dedication and expertise to every engagement.

What We Offer

Our Services

From day-to-day bookkeeping to strategic tax planning, we provide the full range of accounting services your business needs to thrive.

📒

Bookkeeping

Accurate, up-to-date records that give you a clear picture of your financial position. We handle your day-to-day transactions so you can focus on running your business.

💷

Payroll

Fully managed payroll processing, including RTI submissions, PAYE, and employee payslips. We ensure your team is paid accurately and on time, every time.

📋

Tax Returns

Self Assessment tax returns prepared and submitted with care, maximising your legitimate allowances and reliefs while ensuring full HMRC compliance.

🏢

Company Accounts

Statutory year-end accounts prepared to the highest standard and filed with Companies House and HMRC, meeting all legal obligations for limited companies.

📊

VAT Returns

Preparation and submission of VAT returns, including MTD-compliant filings. We manage your VAT obligations and identify planning opportunities to improve cashflow.

🏗️

Company Formation

Guidance on the right structure for your new venture, plus full company incorporation, registered address services, and initial compliance setup.

📈

Management Accounts

Regular management accounts that give you the financial intelligence to make confident decisions, monitor performance, and plan for growth.

🔍

Tax Planning

Proactive advice to structure your affairs in the most tax-efficient way — from dividend planning and capital allowances to pension contributions and beyond.

🤝

Business Advisory

Strategic guidance on cash flow, budgeting, funding, and business planning. We act as a trusted sounding board at every stage of your business journey.
